## Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we do

- Write each project's key findings as plain text, so you do not need an interactive map to understand it.
- Give every image a text description. The site will not build if an image has none.
- Use headings in order, so screen-reader users can jump between sections.
- Use link text that says where the link goes.
- Support light and dark modes with enough contrast in both.

## Known limitations

- Story maps and dashboards are made with ArcGIS and can be hard to use with a screen reader. We link to them as extras; the findings are written out on our pages.
- Some older project reports are PDFs that may not be fully accessible. We can provide another format.
- Some audio recordings do not yet have written descriptions.

## Tell us about a problem

If something on this site does not work for you, email [lasof@wwu.edu](mailto:lasof@wwu.edu). Tell us which page and what happened, and we will fix it or get you the information another way.

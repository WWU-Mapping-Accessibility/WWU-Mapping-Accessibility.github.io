## Students

Take ENVS 421 (GIS IV: Advanced GIS Applications) and work on a MAP project as your final project. You can also do a senior project, independent study or internship with MAP.

## Disabled community members and allies

Tell us what you need to know about getting around campus or Bellingham. Your questions shape what students map next. You can also help us test our maps and written directions.

## Community organizations

Have a mapping question? We may be able to match it with a student team. In 2026, students made a planning map for the Max Higbee Center's downtown outings.

## Instructors and researchers

Interested in accessibility mapping in your own course or research? Get in touch. We are glad to share what has worked. The [MAP library](library.html) collects the research we draw on.

## Contact us

Email Dr. Francisco Laso, MAP coordinator, at [lasof@wwu.edu](mailto:lasof@wwu.edu). Students also run a Discord server, separate from MAP, that is open to people outside WWU. Email us if you would like an invitation.

## Students

Since 2023, students in ENVS 421 (GIS IV: Advanced GIS Applications) have done most of MAP's mapping, along with students doing senior projects, independent studies and internships. Students are credited by name on their project only when they have agreed to it.

## Partners and collaborators

- **Max Higbee Center**, a Bellingham nonprofit that runs recreation programs for people with developmental disabilities
- **Students for Accessible Western (SAW)** and the student advocacy group **This Is Not Accessible (TINA)**
- **Sonic Mapping Collective (SMaC)**, which records the sounds of Sehome Hill Arboretum
